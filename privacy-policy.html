Universal Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 19, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Mohamed El Bouhssini ("we", "our", or "us") handles information across our various mobile applications. Our goal is to provide useful tools while respecting your privacy.

2. Data Collection Based on App Type

A. For Apps with Advertisements (Free Version)

Some of our apps use Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may automatically collect device identifiers (IDFA/AAID), general location data, and usage data to show relevant ads.

B. For Apps with In-App Purchases (Premium Version)

For apps offering premium features or subscriptions (like Nur): All transactions are handled securely by Apple (StoreKit). These apps are designed to collect zero personal data for tracking.

C. For Apps with BOTH Ads & In-App Purchases

In these apps, sections A and B both apply. Choosing to purchase a "Remove Ads" option will disable the advertisement-based data collection.

3. Location Data

Many of our apps (such as Prayer Times or Qibla tools) require location access. This data is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third parties.
