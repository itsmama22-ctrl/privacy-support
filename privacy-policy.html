Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Mohamed El Bouhssini ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our mobile applications (the "Apps"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Information We Collect

We do not directly collect personal information from you. However, our Apps use third-party advertising services that may collect certain information automatically.

Information Collected by Third-Party Services

Our Apps use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect the following information:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
  • Advertising ID: Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android)
  • Location Data: General location information (if you have granted location permissions)
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with ads
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, language settings

How We Use Information

We do not use your personal information directly. The information collected by AdMob is used to:

  • Display relevant advertisements
  • Measure ad performance
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Comply with legal requirements

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

Our Apps use Google AdMob for advertising. AdMob's use of information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review Google's privacy practices:

Google Privacy Policy

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy → Apple Advertising → Limit Ad Tracking
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. Information collected by AdMob may be shared with:

  • Google and its advertising partners
  • Service providers who assist in ad delivery

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. AdMob uses industry-standard security practices to protect data.

Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.
