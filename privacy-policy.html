Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 19, 2026

1. Our Privacy Mission

At the heart of our mission is a simple belief: your data belongs to you. We design our applications with a "Privacy First" approach, ensuring that your spiritual journey remains private and secure.

2. Core Principles

A. Ad-Supported Apps (Free)

Some of our applications use Google AdMob to provide content at no cost. AdMob may collect certain device-specific information (IDFA/AAID) to serve relevant advertisements.

B. Premium Apps (Ad-Free)

Elite applications like Nur are built without any third-party ads or tracking SDKs. These versions are designed to collect zero personal data for marketing purposes.

C. In-App Purchases

All financial transactions are handled securely via Apple StoreKit. We never see or store your payment details or billing address.

3. Localized Data Processing

We believe in decentralized data. Features that require location access (such as prayer times or Qibla direction) are processed locally on your device. No location data is ever transmitted to our servers.

4. Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect the integrity of our systems. Since we don't collect your personal data, your digital footprint remains minimal and secure.
