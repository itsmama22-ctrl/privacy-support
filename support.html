Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

Q: How do I report a bug?

A: Please email us at medlbouhssini@gmail.com with details about the issue, including your device model and iOS version. We'll investigate and fix it as soon as possible.

Q: How do I request a feature?

A: We'd love to hear your ideas! Send us an email at medlbouhssini@gmail.com with your suggestions. Your feedback helps us improve our apps.

Q: Do you collect my personal data?

A: Please see our Privacy Policy for details about data collection. We use Google AdMob for advertising, which may collect certain information as described in our Privacy Policy.

About Ads

Q: Why do I see ads in the apps?

A: Our apps use Google AdMob to display advertisements. This allows us to offer our apps for free. Ads help support development and maintenance of our applications.

Q: Can I remove ads?

A: Currently, our apps are supported by advertising. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy → Apple Advertising → Limit Ad Tracking
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

Q: Are the ads safe?

A: Yes, all ads are served through Google AdMob, which follows strict safety and quality guidelines. We do not control the specific ads shown, but Google filters inappropriate content.

Technical Support

Q: The app is crashing. What should I do?

A: Try these steps:

  • Close and restart the app
  • Update to the latest version from the App Store
  • Restart your device
  • If the issue persists, contact us with details about when the crash occurs

Q: The app won't load data. Why?

A: Make sure you have an active internet connection. Most of our apps require internet to fetch data. Check your Wi-Fi or cellular connection and try again.

Q: Can I use the apps offline?

A: Some features may work offline if data has been cached. However, most features require an internet connection to function properly.
